## Customer Care

Source: https://www.nirc.gov.au/Customer-Service/Customer-care-team

### What you can do at Customer Care (verbatim list)
- pay a Council account (telephone, electricity, rates notices)
- register your motor vehicle
- register for a phone line and ADSL
- purchase a SIM card or eSIM, and top up your phone
- book Rawson Hall or the Supper Room for an event
- lodge Council forms (e.g. pasturage application, dog registration, sale of food licence application)
- lodge rates application forms (e.g. pensioner concession application, change in category application)
- purchase items such as sand, compost and mulch
- pay rates, electricity, telecom and lighterage charges
- hire Council equipment
- read the Council Meeting Agendas and Minutes
- ask for directory assistance for local landline and mobile numbers

### How requests are logged, referred and tracked
- "Customer Care will try and respond to any email enquiries directly. If the team is unable to answer your inquiry, they will pass this to another member of staff within Council to respond. When your inquiry is passed to another staff member, it is loaded into a Customer Resolution Management (CRM) system, and you will receive a confirmation of this by email. You can check the progress of your CRM by phoning Customer Care with the reference number provided in the email." — https://www.nirc.gov.au/Customer-Service/Customer-care-team
- When you give Customer Care an email address, you receive an email with a reference number. To check progress, phone Customer Care and quote the reference number. Customer Care can load "a customer request to a department on your behalf". — https://www.nirc.gov.au/Your-council/About-Council/Customer-service-charter
- Customer Service Charter PDF (https://www.nirc.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/your-council/documents/customer_service_charter.pdf):
  - Phone messages: response **within 2 working days**.
  - Emails, faxes and letters: response **within 10 working days**. Website enquiries also get a response within 10 working days.
  - CRM handles "requests for specific services such as dangerous pot holes and falling tree limbs as well as requests for information". Customer Care logs a CRM to the relevant department, which must act "within Councils defined service response time of **10 working days**".
  - Complaints go to the Complaints Officer: regionalcouncil@nirc.gov.nf, by post, or in person.
- Current CRM status: see `norfolk-context.md` (Q2 report item 3.6.5). The report says Council is looking at alternatives to Civica CRM.

## Works Depot

Source: https://www.nirc.gov.au/Infrastructure/Infrastructure-Services/Works-depot

- Responsible for construction and maintenance of Council buildings, the quarry and the weighbridge. Under a service delivery arrangement with the Commonwealth, it also maintains the KAVHA buildings and the landing places at Kingston and Cascade.
- Sections: Works Management, Building Maintenance, Cascade – sale of rock, Grounds, Lighterage, Mechanics, Roads, Stores.
- Responsibilities include:
  - building construction, improvements and maintenance
  - community assistance for burials
  - quarry rock removal and weighing
  - grounds and sporting grounds
  - erecting marquees and auxiliary tents
  - mowing (Puppy's Point Reserve, Anson Bay Reserve, ANZCAN Station, Bicentennial Centre, Waste Management Reserve)
  - ship-to-shore cargo and passengers (lighterage)
  - preventative maintenance, mechanical repairs, engineering and metal fabrication
  - vehicle detailing, panel beating and spray painting
  - sign/guide-post maintenance
  - road maintenance (patching, culverts, water runoffs, private sealing works)
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 7.00am–3.00pm.
- Address: Works Depot, New Cascade Road, Norfolk Island 2899. Phone: +6723 22244. Email: customercare@nirc.gov.nf.
- Sub-pages: Supply of Rock (https://www.nirc.gov.au/Infrastructure/Infrastructure-Services/Works-depot/Rock-supply) and Lighterage (https://www.nirc.gov.au/Infrastructure/Infrastructure-Services/Works-depot/Lighterage). These were **not fetched**.

**Driveway / access / entrance (form 209).** Fields marked * are mandatory:
- Name of applicant*, Address*, Email*, Phone*
- Name of Road*
- Location of proposed access/entrance (Portion number of property)*
- Size of area and type of proposed access or entrance*
- Description of land/s adjacent to road reserve (Portion numbers)*
- Attached sketch showing location, dimensions and levels
- Acknowledgements: (a) no work until approved by NIRC; (b) applicant meets costs of restoring road pavement and other remedial work
- Business/Company name and ABN/ACN (if applicable)
- Signature* and Date
- Lodgement: mail, email or in person (Council Bicentennial Complex, 39 Taylors Road)
- "What now": an Officer responds **within 10 working days**
- Official use: Receiving Officer name, signature and date; Approved/not approved; Date applicant notified
- No fee is stated on the form.

**Pipeline / conduit crossing (form 212).** Same structure as form 209:
- Road name*
- Location (Portion number)*
- Size and type of pipe or conduit*
- Adjacent land (portion numbers)*
- Sketch
- Same two acknowledgements
- Business/Company name, ABN/ACN*
- Signature of Applicant & Position Held*
- Response within 10 working days
- No fee is stated on the form.
