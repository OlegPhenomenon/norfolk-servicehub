## 1. Application for Hire of Council Plant / Equipment
URL: https://www.nirc.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/infrastructure/documents/application_for_hire_of_council_equipment.pdf. Footer: "Updated 07 February 2018", 3 pages.

Fill in BLOCK LETTERS; fields marked * are mandatory. "The hirer will be invoiced by the Norfolk Island Regional Council".

**Privacy statutory references:**
- Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the APPs
- s739 Local Government Act 1993 (NSW)(NI)
- NSW Model Privacy Management Plan for Local Government
- Privacy Code of Practice for Local Government (NSW)

The same privacy text appears on every NIRC form below.

**Fields in order:**
1. Name of applicant*
2. Address*
3. Email*
4. Phone* (Home; Work/Mobile)
5. Plant/Equipment to be hired*
6. Where the plant/equipment is required (e.g. Road, property, area)*
7. Date and time hiring commenced*
8. Date and time hiring was completed*
9. Purpose of hiring (brief detail of work)*
10. Business/Company Name (if applicable); ABN/ACN number
11. Applicant Signature; Date
12. Indemnity declaration: the hirer indemnifies NIRC; equipment is supplied "as is"; use is at the hirer's own risk
13. Lodgement details:
    - Mail: PO Box 95
    - Email: customercare@nirc.gov.nf
    - In person: Customer Care Team, "9 New Cascade Road" (old address)
    - "What now: You will be invoiced"
14. **OFFICIAL USE ONLY**:
    - Receiving Officer – Name, Date, Signature
    - Plant/Equipment hired
    - Number of hours plant/equipment hired
    - Hourly Rate: $
    - Amount to Invoice: $

**Conditions of Hiring (page 3):**
1. Rates as fixed by NIRC from time to time.
2. Operator overtime is charged in addition.
3. The rate includes fuel, oil and the operator's ordinary-time wages.
4. The hire period runs from when the equipment leaves the "Local Services Depot" until it returns. If the hirer stands the equipment down, the charge runs to the operator's return or until the equipment is packed up. If operation resumes within 4 hours, the hire counts as continuous.
5. Notify accidents and damage immediately.
6. The hirer pays for damage repairs (fair wear and tear excepted).
7. Rate as per the Operational Plan Fees and Charges Schedule for the current financial year.
8. Indemnity.

No fee amount is printed on the form.

## 2. Application for Planning Certificates – Section 98 Planning Act 2002 (NI)
URL: https://www.nirc.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/planning-development/documents/application_for_planning_certificate_section_98_planning_act_2002.pdf. "Updated July 2024", 1 page. Header: ABN 6010 3855 713.

- Purpose: apply for a Planning Certificate under **s98 Planning Act 2002 (NI)**.
- Submit by email to planning@nirc.gov.nf, or in person at Customer Care, **together with a copy of the payment receipt**.

**Fields in order:**
1. Applicant details: Name, Address, Email, Phone
2. Property details: **Lot**, **Section**, **Portion**; Owners Name; Street Address
3. Signature of Applicant; Date

**Fee:**
- The form prints **$181.13**, with a reference to the 2025-26 fees PDF (`…/v/3/corporate-and-finance/documents/nirc-fc-25-26-24072025.pdf`).
- The FY 2026-27 schedule also lists Planning Certificate **$181.13** (fees PDF above, p.16).
- Note: "Certificates issued are for each address which may include more than one adjoining lot."

No office-use section on this form.

## 3. Application for Modification of Development Approval and/or Building Approval
URL: https://www.nirc.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/planning-development/documents/application_to_modify_development_and_or_building_approval_form_18_03_24.pdf. Footer: "Updated 12 March 2024", 9 pages.

- Statutory basis: **Planning Act 2002 (NI)**, Division 4 (modifications to development approvals); **Building Act 2002 (NI)**, Division 2 (modifications to building approvals).
- Privacy note: permitted use/development applications are notified and made available for inspection. Permissible-with-consent applications are published in the Gazette for comment. Details are kept in a public Register.
- Notes: consult Planning/Building staff first (typically 15–30 minutes). After lodgement you are told whether the application is complete enough to be accepted for assessment. After assessment you receive a **Notice of Decision**.

**Fields in order:**
- Header: APPLICATION NO.; ORIGINAL APPLICATION NUMBER
1. **Applicant's details** (may be an agent acting for the landowner):
   - Name Applicant 1 and 2 (First/Last)
   - Postal Address; Phone; Mob.; Email(s)
   - Signature Applicant 1 and 2
2. **Landowner's details** (if not the applicant):
   - Name ×2; Postal Address; Phone; Mob.; Email
   - Signatures of all landowners. This is the landowner's consent to lodge the modification only.
3. **Property description**:
   - Address
   - Portion No., Lot No., Section No., Land Area (2 rows)
   - Land Tenure: Freehold / Crown Lease / Vacant Crown Land / Road Reserve / Un-alienated Crown Land
   - Zoning: Rural / Rural Residential / Residential / Mixed Use / Business / Light Industry / Industry / Open Space / Conservation / Special Use / Airport / Roads
   - Attach a copy of the Title Search
   - "What is the land currently used for?"
4. **Type(s) of use/development/building** (tick):
   - Residential (Tourist Accommodation Units; Dual Occupancy; Dwelling House; Multiple Dwelling)
   - Commercial
   - Industrial
   - Home Industry/Occupation
   - Alterations and Additions
   - Ancillary structures (Garage, Verandah, Shed)
   - Change of Use
   - Subdivision
   - Advertising Structure/Signage
   - Community
   - Infrastructure
   - Earthworks
   - Other
   - Notes: activities in **Clause 101 Norfolk Island Plan 2002** need no development approval. Activities in **Schedule 1 Building Regulations 2002 (NI)** need no building approval.
5. **Description of proposed modification** (tick, then describe the expected impact):
   - minor error, misdescription or miscalculation
   - modification to condition(s)
   - change of approval lapse date (give the proposed date and reasons)
   - any other modification
6. **Substantially the same use/development?**
   - Describe the modified proposal, including all modifications since the original approval.
   - Describe changes in the external environment since the original approval.
7. **Application fees**:
   - Fees are in the Annual Operational Plan, based on the estimated cost of building and works.
   - For building work, Council currently assesses at **$1200 per square metre**.
   - "Total estimated cost of building and works $"
8. **Other approvals** (tick):
   - EPBC Act 1999 (Cth)
   - Crown Lands Act 1996 (NI)
   - Local Government Act 1993 (NSW)(NI)
   - Trees Act 1997 (NI)
   - Public Reserves Act 1997 (NI)
   - Subdivision Act 2002 (NI)
   - Tourist Accommodation Act 1984 (NI)
   - Sale of Food Act 1950 (NI)
   - Liquor Act 2005 (NI)
   - Heritage Act 2002 (NI)
   - Roads Act 2002 (NI)
   - Other
9. **Supporting information**: list attachments (plans, drawings, photos)
- **Lodgement**:
  - Mail: PO Box 95, Norfolk Island 2899
  - Email: planning@nirc.gov.nf
  - In person: Customer Care, Bicentennial Complex, 39 Taylors Road, Burnt Pine
  - "What now": a Council Officer responds **within 10 working days** on whether the application can be accepted for assessment
- **Official use only**:
  - Receiving Officer; Date
  - Adequacy for acceptance: satisfactory Yes/No; additional information required
  - Planning Act: accepted as a Modification of DA Yes/No
  - Building Act: accepted as a Modification of BA Yes/No
  - Application acceptance: Officer; Date
- **Internal use only (not for publication)**:
  - Fee Paid $; Receipt No.; Receipt Date
  - Modification Application No.
  - Combined / DA-only / BA-only modification
  - Original DA and/or BA number
  - Modification Application Fee advice attached

**Fee (FY 2026-27 schedule, p.18):**
- Basic Modification (a request to modify the lapse date): **$250.00**
- Standard Modification (all others): equivalent to the 'Building and Works' application fee calculation. See `planning-process.md`.
