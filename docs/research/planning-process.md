## DA/BA form — extra field groups not in the modify form ([DABA])
- **Activities**: erecting/altering a building; temporary building/use; subdividing; demolition; change of use or BCA classification.
- **Description of proposal**: lots, dwellings, bedrooms, seats, operating hours, production volume.
- **Use, development and building details**:
  - Gross floor area (new and existing); total roof area; bedrooms; maximum height
  - Setbacks (front, rear, side, side)
  - Wall, floor, roof and frame materials and colours
- **Water**: existing, new and total tank litres (DCP No.2).
- **Wastewater**: sewer connection or onsite system, with tank litres.
- **Signage**: DCP No.4.
- **Earthworks**: m² and m³.
- **Swimming pool**.
- **Builder's details**.
- **Internal use**: assessment fee and receipt; stage A–E inspection fees paid; additional inspections.
