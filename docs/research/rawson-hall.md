## Booking process
1. Get and read the Conditions of Hire ([APP]).
2. Download and complete the "Application for Hire of a Council Premises" form. All fields are mandatory; use BLOCK LETTERS (Page, [APP]).
3. Submit it to Customer Care with any additional documents and the **bond payment**. "A bond is payable at the time of booking." (Page)
4. A booking is accepted once the application form is complete, staff confirm the premises is available, and the fees/charges and bond are received ([COND] "Bookings").
5. Lodgement ([APP]): mail to PO Box 95, Norfolk Island 2899; email customercare@nirc.gov.nf; or in person at the Customer Care Team. The form lists the address "9 New Cascade Road", which appears outdated. The current Customer Care office is the Bicentennial Complex, 39 Taylors Rd.

## Bond / deposit rules
- The bond is paid at booking (Page). The bond is $250, refundable ([FEES]).
- If Council has to remove rubbish or items, the hirer pays a cost-recovery fee and loses any bond paid ([COND] "Cleaning and rubbish disposal").
- A Security/Cleaning Deposit "may be required in certain circumstances and at the discretion of NIRC" ([COND]).
- Advertising on trees, footpaths or external walls → "hirer's deposits being forfeited" ([COND]).
- If Council cancels because of an emergency or a Council function, any return of the deposit is at Council's discretion ([COND]).

## Cleaning / setup rules ([COND])
- Leave the premises clean and tidy. Mop the floors and take rubbish away. Recycle recyclables. Avoid polystyrene and plastic.
- All changes to furniture, fittings, fixtures and moveable walls are made by NIRC staff.
- Decorations go only on the approved hooks. No tape, blu-tack or pins on painted surfaces. No suspended objects unless approved.
- No confetti, rose petals, rice or metallic sprinkles. No animals except guide and hearing dogs. No chewing gum.
- Remove the hirer's property **by 12 noon on the first business day after the hire**. Return keys by the same deadline. If keys are lost, the hirer pays for new keys or locks.
- Loud music stops **by 10pm** unless agreed. The venue is smoke-free.
- Alcohol: the hirer applies to the Registrar of Liquor Licencing with at least 28 days' notice. An RSA-certified server is required. Security may be required, e.g. for bands or where alcohol is sold.
- Public liability: the certificate of currency must show at least **$20,000,000** and note NIRC for its respective rights and interests (required for clubs, associations, commercial hirers, etc.).
- The hirer or a responsible representative must be present for the whole hire. Children under 16 must be supervised.
- Activities outside the building (grounds, footpath, car park) need a separate application and an Event Management Application Form.

## Cancellation rules ([COND] "Cancellations")
- Cancellations are accepted only with **more than 7 days' notice for meetings**, and **30 days' notice for Weddings, Concerts, Stage Shows or Balls**. Later cancellations incur the cancellation fee (amount not found).
- NIRC may cancel if an advance payment is not made or honoured. It may also cancel for breach, likely damage, or objectionable use, and forfeit the payment. NIRC may cancel with or without notice for an Emergency or Council Function.
- "Responsible Officer" = NIRC Manager Commercial Services, unless advised otherwise.
